# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We aim to make KONGCHAT 2.0 a welcoming, respectful, collaborative community.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Accept feedback professionally.
- Focus on ideas and technical work.
- Respect privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment or discrimination
- Threats or personal attacks
- Publishing private information
- Deliberate disruption

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this code.
