# Sponsorship

KONGCHAT 2.0 is an independent open-source messaging platform.

## What Funding Supports
- Infrastructure and hosting
- Development tools
- Testing and security review
- Documentation
- Community infrastructure

## Transparency
Funding links will be published only after sponsor eligibility and account setup are confirmed. Funding received and its use should be reported transparently.

Sponsorship does not guarantee features, influence over security decisions, or preferential access.
